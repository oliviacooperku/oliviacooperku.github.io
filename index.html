About Me
Hi, I'm Olivia.

I'm a 20-year-old journalism student at the University of Kansas with a passion for storytelling that goes beyond the headline. I believe the best journalism feels like a conversation — honest, human, and worth your time.

My focus spans three areas: long-form storytelling, video production, and documentary photography. Whether I'm behind a camera or at a keyboard, I'm chasing the same thing — a story that makes you feel something.
